# Origen de las imágenes de ejemplo

Las seis imágenes de `img/demo/` se generaron con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Después se redujeron y convirtieron a WebP para la web. Ninguna fotografía documenta un evento real.

## Prompts

- **noche-arte-plaza.webp**: Editorial photograph of a fictional municipal cultural evening in a small Andalusian village plaza at blue hour, warm string lights, modest outdoor stage, people seen from behind, whitewashed buildings, wide 16:9, no logos or text.
- **noche-arte-cartel.webp**: Vertical illustrated Andalusian evening poster with lanterns, stars and indigo and amber palette. Exact text: “NOCHE DE ARTE”, “GILENA”, “21 NOV · 18:30”. No extra text or logos.
- **ruta-olivar.webp**: Editorial photograph of a fictional family nature walk among olive groves and low hills near Gilena, autumn morning, walkers seen from behind, wide 16:9, no text or logos.
- **ruta-olivar-cartel.webp**: Vertical illustrated olive grove and hill walking poster, autumn sunlight. Exact text: “RUTA DEL OLIVAR”, “28 NOV · 09:30”, “GILENA”. No extra text or logos.
- **olivos-detalle.webp**: Secondary editorial gallery photograph of ancient olive trees and a dry-stone path, late autumn sun, distant small walkers, wide 4:3, no text or logos.
- **taller-pan.webp**: Editorial photograph of adults’ and children’s hands preparing traditional bread and olive oil at a community center table, natural light, wide 16:9, no identifiable faces, text or logos.
